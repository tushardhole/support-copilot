# Getting Started with Acme SaaS

## Account Setup

1. Visit [app.acme.io](https://app.acme.io) and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your work email and create a password (min. 12 characters).
3. Confirm your email via the link we send you.
4. Complete your profile: name, company, timezone.

## Inviting Team Members

Go to **Settings → Team** and click **Invite Member**.
Enter their email. They'll receive an invite valid for 7 days.
Roles available: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Member**, **Viewer**.

## First Project

1. Click **+ New Project** from the dashboard.
2. Choose a template or start blank.
3. Add your first task by clicking **+ Add Task** inside the project.

## Billing & Plans

| Plan | Price | Users | Storage |
|------|-------|-------|---------|
| Starter | Free | 3 | 5 GB |
| Pro | $29/mo | 15 | 50 GB |
| Business | $99/mo | Unlimited | 500 GB |

Billing is monthly by default; annual plans get 20% off.
Invoices are emailed on the 1st of each month.
